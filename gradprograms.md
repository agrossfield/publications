We take graduate students from multiple graduate programs both at University of Rochester Medical Center and University of Rochester River Campus. If you are interested in joining the lab, please apply to one of the following programs:

- [Biochemistry, Structural and Computational Biology](https://www.urmc.rochester.edu/education/graduate/phd/biophysics)
- [Biochemistry and Molecular Biology](https://www.urmc.rochester.edu/education/graduate/phd/biochemistry)
- [Chemistry](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/chm/graduate/index.html)
