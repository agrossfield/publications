title: Cysteine derivatives as therapeutics for opioid overdose 
---

For the last several years, we have been collaborating with the lab of Dr.
Stephen J. Lewis of Case Western Reserve University to develop cysteine
derivatives as therapeutics for opioid overdose. Lewis and coworkers have shown
that cysteine derivatives can reverse the effects of opioids in rats, while my group has worked on unconvering the mechanism of action. We have focused on the interaction of these compounds, e.g. D-cysteine ethyl ester, with beta-arrestins, proteins that regulate the internalization and recycling of GPCRs like opioid receptors.  We have combined conventional molecular dynamics simulations with alchemical ligand binding free energy calculations.
